    💡 Image-Taking Tips

    For better identification results, follow these tips:

    ☀ Use good lighting Natural light works best.
    🍎 Capture one fruit only Avoid multiple fruits in one frame.
    ▣ Keep the fruit fully visible Show the entire fruit in the image.
    ▢ Use a plain background Avoid cluttered or distracting backgrounds.
    📷 Avoid blurry images Ensure the photo is sharp and clear.
    🔍 Take the photo from a close and clear angle Get close and focused for better results.
